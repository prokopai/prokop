### Added

- **Surfaces and accents**: Appearance now has three choices instead of a list of schemes. Mode is light, dark or system. Surface sets the grays: Graphite is neutral, Warm, Ocean, Moss and Mauve add a hint of color, and Carbon is a darker dark mode. Accent sets the one color used for selection, the on switch, charts and the tinted corner of the window: Blue, Teal, Green, Amber or Violet. A scheme you picked before becomes the nearest surface and accent on the first start, nothing to redo.

- **Agent and model as two chips in the session header**: The one control that held model, effort and agent behind three cells is two chips at the right of the header. The agent chip lists your agents with their descriptions and the model each one pins; in a workspace that belongs to an agent the chip shows that agent with a lock. The model chip opens one list: your recent picks on top, then one row per installed runtime (Prokop, Claude Code, Codex) that opens into its models, so long catalogs stay short, and an effort row at the bottom for the chosen model. Typing searches across runtimes, providers and models at once. On a phone the same lists open as a sheet from the bottom.

### Changed

- **A new shell**: The window is a shaded field with the panels floating on it: the docks and the center are rounded panels with a soft edge, and the gaps between them are the resize handles. Dock tabs are pills with the current one lifted, the open session is a tinted pill, and menus, popovers and dialogs blur what is behind them. Text is one step denser: 13px body with 12px detail and 15px titles, and buttons, fields and rows are 28px. The loading screen follows the same layout.

- **Settings is a tab, not a dialog**: Settings opens in the center like files and pull requests, with its pages listed at the left, and on a phone the list comes first. The header button opens it in place, from the overview too. Every page is groups of rows: the label and a sentence on the left, the control sized to its content on the right, a switch for every yes or no. Pages that list things of one kind (MCP servers, providers, agents, prompts, formats, devices, permissions) show one row per thing with its icon, name, one status line and its controls; a row opens its editor in place. Add, Sync, Pair, Refresh and search sit in the page header next to the workspace switcher. Push notifications are one switch with the state in the row text.

- **Dialogs and forms on the same rows**: The scheduler, rename, overview group, additional paths, file create, rename and delete dialogs and the pull request form use the same label-left, control-right rows as settings, with a plain Cancel and one primary action in the footer.

- **Pull requests as rows**: The list is two-line rows like the session list, with the open one highlighted and "Review requested" leading the status line. A pull request's overview has Merge, Reviewers and Checks as groups at the right, the merge method as a row, one primary action, and votes shown only when cast. The description is prose with edit on hover; comments and threads are hairline-divided boxes with the thread path as the label.

- **Usage and providers at a glance**: Every usage group and every provider row leads with a tile carrying the provider's mark, tinted for brands with a known color, so accounts can be found by shape before reading. Usage lays its groups out in two columns once the panel is wide enough.

- **One color for cues, one for buttons**: Selection, status dots and the current item use the accent color; buttons keep a single fill per surface and the rest are outlined. Links are underlined text. Before this, in the neutral scheme every dot, selection and status came out white.

- **Notices come from one place**: Update ready, reconnecting, attention asks and action errors all show as the same kind of notice in the corner, each updating in place instead of stacking. A newer server release is one sentence, the same in the notice, the header dot and the settings footer.

- **The message box stands on its own**: The box where you type used to take the same gray as the conversation behind it, and clicking into it showed almost no change. It has its own slightly lighter fill with a thin edge, and clicking into it lifts the box with a soft shadow instead of drawing a thick outline. The checkout, permission and format controls under it are chips with their value written out; in a narrow pane on a wide window they show just their icons, as they already did on a phone, instead of overflowing. Your own messages take the accent tint, and every line of the conversation sits on the same 24px rhythm.

- **One corner shape per kind of thing**: Buttons, tabs and chips share one shape, rows and menus another, the message box matches your message bubbles, and dialogs and the command palette take the same corners as the panels around them.

- **Installed app title bar matches the window**: The installed app's title bar used to take the old flat background color, a shade lighter than the field the panels sit on. It takes the field's own color now, so the bar and the window read as one surface.

### Fixed

- **Phone views lost their background**: On a phone, Settings, the file explorer, the editor and the other side views showed the window's tinted backdrop behind their content and the explorer's ignored rows looked like stripes. Every view paints the plain background again, as the chat already did.
